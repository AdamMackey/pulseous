Checking Claude's Pulse

Pulseous keeps Claude's service status in Safari's toolbar: a checkmark when all is well, an X when it isn't, and the full picture one click away.

Add to Firefox · Free Add to Edge · Free

▶︎  Watch the demo

Free for Firefox and Edge · Safari and Chrome coming soon · No account needed

Readable at a glance

A ✓ on the toolbar icon while everything is operational. It turns to ✕ the moment any service is degraded, down or in maintenance.

The whole picture

Every Claude service with its state, any open incident with its latest update, and scheduled maintenance, each linking to status.claude.com.

Asks for nothing

No website access, no account, no servers, no analytics. It reads Claude's public status feed every five minutes and nothing else.

See it in action

Thirty seconds, no sound: the checkmark in Safari's toolbar, the popup with every service, then through to the status page.

Set up in a minute

  1. Open Pulseous once from your Applications folder.
  2. In Safari, choose Settings → Extensions and tick Pulseous.
  3. That's it. The pulse sits in the toolbar; click it any time for the detail.

Questions?

The support page covers what the badge means, why the icon is grey, and what to do if it says the status page can't be reached. Or email support@pulseous.app.
