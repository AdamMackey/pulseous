Privacy policy

Pulseous for Safari · effective 16 September 2026

The short version: Pulseous collects no personal data. It has no website access, no servers, no analytics, no accounts and no tracking. Everything it reads stays on your Mac.

What Pulseous accesses

Pulseous reads one thing: the public status feed for Claude's services, at https://status.claude.com/api/v2/summary.json. This is the same information shown on status.claude.com, published by Anthropic for anyone to read. Pulseous requests it every five minutes while Safari is running, and when you open the popup. The request carries no login, cookies or identifiers.

Pulseous asks Safari for access to no websites. It cannot read, change or observe any page you visit, on any site.

What Pulseous stores

Pulseous keeps the most recent status result in Safari's local extension storage on your Mac: the state of each service, any open incidents and maintenance windows, and the time of the last check. If a check fails, it also keeps the error message. Nothing is stored anywhere else. Removing the extension deletes it.

Where your data goes

Nowhere. Pulseous sends no information to the developer or to any third party. Its only network request is the read of the public status feed described above. It contains no analytics, crash reporting or advertising code.

Third-party services

status.claude.com is operated by Anthropic, PBC, using Atlassian Statuspage. Reading it is subject to their terms. Pulseous is not affiliated with Anthropic or Atlassian.

Children

Pulseous is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone.
